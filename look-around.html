The ESP32 bench

An ESP32 DevKit on a breadboard with a DHT22 and an OLED, a second board in a printed case wired to soil probes in a seedling tray, and a relay driving a little pump. The same kind of kit as the per-zone soil, climate and pump nodes in my AgroFlow project, reporting over MQTT.
